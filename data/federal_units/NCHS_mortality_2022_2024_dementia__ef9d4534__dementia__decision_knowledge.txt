WHAT THIS FILE ESTABLISHES
Counts of U.S. death records whose underlying cause is Alzheimer's disease (G30), vascular dementia (F01) or unspecified dementia (F03), for 2022, 2023 and 2024. The underlying-cause field is positions 146-149 in the official layout (2021 to 2024 documentation).

DECISION BOUNDARY
Death records, not people living with dementia. Only the underlying cause is counted, so deaths where dementia was a contributing cause are missed. Not a measure of caregiving, cost or nonfatal burden. The F01 and F03 codes may be used differently by certifiers, so changes from year to year are not explained here.
