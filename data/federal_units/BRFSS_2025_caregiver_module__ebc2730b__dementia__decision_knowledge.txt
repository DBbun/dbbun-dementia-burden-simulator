PROCESSING AND INTERPRETATION STATUS
Derived summary computed by script from a public federal file. Calculated findings are separate from any extracted passages. This package is not independently scientifically reviewed or automatically approved for simulation.

WHAT THIS FILE ESTABLISHES
The 2025 BRFSS caregiver module was answered in 27 areas (AL, AK, AZ, DE, FL, HI, ID, IN, IA, KS, LA, MN, MO, MT, NH, NM, ND, OR, PA, RI, SC, TN, TX, VT, WA, WV, PR). It counts adults who provided regular care in the past 30 days, and asks caregivers what health problem the person has, how many hours a week, how long, and which tasks. Weighted percentages are computed from the public file with the final weight and are shown for all asking areas and for Puerto Rico alone, with approximate standard errors. Hours are in three bands only (under 20, 20 to 39, 40 or more per week).

DECISION BOUNDARY
Not a national estimate: only the listed areas asked the module. Self-report, not a diagnosis. The hours bands are 40 or more, 20 to 39 and under 20; they cannot be compared with a paper that reports mean hours or a different cut point without saying so. No dollar costs, income loss or access barriers are measured. The Puerto Rico dementia-caregiver subset is small; read the interval, not the point value.

MISSING EVIDENCE / NEXT ANALYSIS
Burden scales, depression and respite use are not in the module. Weighted estimates by age or sex are not computed here.

DO NOT USE FOR
Treating a federal figure as the simulator's own input, comparing it with a paper whose population, definition or period differs without saying so, or filling absent costs or effects with invented values.

Analysis is source-backed but has not had independent scientific review. No modeled savings or assumed effects are supplied.
