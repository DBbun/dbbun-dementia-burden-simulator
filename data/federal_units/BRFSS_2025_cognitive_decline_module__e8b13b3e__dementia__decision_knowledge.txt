WHAT THIS FILE ESTABLISHES
The 2025 BRFSS cognitive decline module (adults 45 and over) was answered in 16 areas (CT, DE, GA, HI, IL, IN, KS, KY, MO, OR, RI, TN, TX, WA, WI, PR). It asks about memory or thinking difficulties that are happening more often or getting worse, and follow-up items about worry, talking to a provider, giving up chores and interference with work or social life.

DECISION BOUNDARY
Not a national estimate. Self-report: this is not a dementia diagnosis and does not count people with dementia. Follow-up items are asked only of those who said yes to the first item.
