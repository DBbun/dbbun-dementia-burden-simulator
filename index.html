DBbun Dementia Burden Simulator

Working title. Software test copy. Draft categories, not validated.
Read this first. This page describes a research prototype. It is not medical advice, not a clinical tool and not a validated measure. Nothing here has been checked by a clinician or an ethics board. Figures shown come from public federal files and are copied with their source, year and caveats. Paper results in the library are drafts.

What it does

People who live with dementia and the family members who care for them carry burdens that are spread across many papers and often reported in different units. The prototype reads an open-access dementia paper and lists the burdens it reports for patients and for caregivers, each one with the exact sentence, the page or table where it appears, and a check that the number really occurs in the paper's text.

  1. Read. The paper's text is split by page so every finding can point back to its location.
  2. Extract. A language model proposes findings, each tagged with one of 15 draft burden categories, who it concerns (patient, caregiver or both) and who reported it.
  3. Check. A separate script confirms that each quoted sentence and each number occurs in the paper. Findings that fail are marked, not hidden.

The tool is adapted from DBbun's earlier software for a different federal challenge on Lyme disease and Long COVID cost of illness. Its categories, prompts and page were rewritten for dementia and caregivers; the code that reads papers and runs the checks was reused.

Worked examples

Three draft simulators, each built from one open-access (CC BY 4.0) dementia paper, are in the simulator library. Each shows the model's findings next to the checker's result. They are drafts: a person has not yet reviewed every label, and burden categories are not assigned yet.

How it is being tested

  • A pilot on two or three open-access dementia papers. A person codes each paper by hand using the written codebook before looking at any tool output.
  • Targets set in advance: at least 95% of extracted numbers correct, 100% of locators valid, at least 90% of category labels correct.
  • The codebook is a draft until frozen; any later change is dated, explained and tied to the papers affected.
  • Findings the checker cannot confirm are reported as such.

AI use

Software and page text were written with Claude (Anthropic): Claude Sonnet 5.5 (model ID claude-sonnet-5-5). The extraction step in the prototype calls claude-sonnet-5. The draft codebook was reviewed with four language models, treated as opinions and not as evidence. A person made all design decisions and reviews the output.
