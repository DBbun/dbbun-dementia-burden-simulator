This is an audio overview of one draft simulator: Dementia Caregiver Burden, Hours, and Depressive Symptoms, from Puerto Rico. The voice is synthetic. It is Kokoro, an open-source text-to-speech model. This is an educational what-if model, not medical advice.

The simulator is built from one open-access paper, published in The Journals of Gerontology, Series B, in twenty twenty-six. It is a survey of one hundred and one dementia caregivers in Puerto Rico, from the PREHCO Wave three study, collected in twenty twenty-one and twenty twenty-two.

What the paper reports. Caregivers gave very high amounts of unpaid care. On average it was about one hundred hours a week, and about half of the caregivers reported more than one hundred hours a week. The paper also reports that higher caregiver burden went along with higher caregiver depressive symptoms, a correlation of zero point seven two. General social support did not weaken that link in this sample.

What you can change. The simulator has five inputs: weekly caregiving hours, a standard full-time workweek used as a benchmark, a caregiver burden score on a zero to twenty-four scale, a count of the patient's behavioral and psychological symptoms, and the burden and depression correlation. It turns them into annual caregiving hours, hours above a full-time workweek, a depressive symptom score linked to a given burden level, and two scores shown as a percent of the maximum scale score.

There are three ready-made scenarios. One sets weekly hours to sixty-seven and a half, a midpoint of the paper's thirty-five to one hundred hour bracket. One sets hours to one hundred, the paper's threshold for its highest group. The third swaps in a correlation of zero point five two, from the wider literature that the authors cite, to see how much the result depends on this sample's unusually strong link.

What it cannot tell you. The paper reports no dollar costs, no income loss and no insurance or access-barrier data, so none appear here. The results are transformations of the paper's own numbers. They are not a cost estimate and not a causal effect, and they should not be generalized beyond this sample.

Draft status. A script confirmed that the numbers and quotes occur in the paper. A person read the findings against the paper. The burden categories are not yet assigned, and the codebook behind them is frozen for a pilot but not validated.
